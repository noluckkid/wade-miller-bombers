Key Matchups to Watch

Labour Day Classic & Banjo Bowl

The traditional rivalry with Saskatchewan returns with back-to-back games: September 6 in Regina (Labour Day Classic) and September 12 at home (Banjo Bowl). These games are among the most intense in Canadian football.

Orange Shirt Game — September 25

The Bombers host Toronto in recognition of the National Day for Truth and Reconciliation. A meaningful annual tradition honouring residential school survivors and their families.

Thanksgiving Weekend — October 10

The Bombers host Calgary on Thanksgiving Saturday. A marquee matchup as Winnipeg looks to secure playoff positioning in the final stretch.

TV & Streaming

All Blue Bombers games are broadcast nationally in Canada on TSN (Sportsnetwork) and RDS for French-language coverage. U.S. viewers can stream via CFL+ and select games air on CBS Sports Network.

Blackout restrictions apply for home games in the Winnipeg market. All games are available for streaming on the CFL+ app for out-of-market viewers.
