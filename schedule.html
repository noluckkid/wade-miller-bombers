2026 Theme Nights

Every home game at Princess Auto Stadium gets its own vibe — from Santa hats in July to a full-blown 90's revival. Here's the full rundown.

🚀 Season Opener — Jun 5 @ Calgary

First crack at the Stamps on the road. New season, same energy.

🏟️ Home Opener — Jun 11 vs Hamilton

Back at Princess Auto. The tarp comes off and the sellout streak keeps rolling.

☀️ Summer Kickoff — Jun 25 vs Edmonton

Longest day of the year vibes, Indigenous Peoples Day celebration, and the Elks in town.

💙 Mental Health & First Responders — Jul 10 vs Toronto

Honouring the people who show up when it counts. (Toronto's "home" game, moved to WPG for FIFA.)

🎄 Christmas in July — Jul 24 vs Calgary

Santa hats at 30°C. The most wonderful time of the… summer?

🕶️ 90's Night — Jul 30 vs BC

Dial-up, scrunchies, and the Lions. Expect a decade-appropriate halftime playlist.

🤠 Country Night — Aug 14 vs Ottawa

Boots, hats, and the Redblacks. Y'all ready for some football?

🎖️ Armed Forces Appreciation — Aug 28 vs Montreal

Saluting the men and women in uniform as the Alouettes roll into town.

🏈 Labour Day Classic — Sep 6 @ Regina

The most intense rivalry in Canadian football. Labour Day means Riders week.

🪕 Banjo Bowl — Sep 12 vs Saskatchewan

Revenge game six days later. Banjos out, sellout crowd, all gas.

🧡 Orange Shirt Day — Sep 25 vs Toronto

Honouring residential school survivors. Every child matters.

🦃 Thanksgiving Game — Oct 10 vs Calgary

Turkey, stuffing, and a Stampeders showdown on the long weekend.

TV & Streaming

All Blue Bombers games are broadcast nationally in Canada on TSN (Sportsnetwork) and RDS for French-language coverage. U.S. viewers can stream via CFL+ and select games air on CBS Sports Network.

Blackout restrictions apply for home games in the Winnipeg market. All games are available for streaming on the CFL+ app for out-of-market viewers.
