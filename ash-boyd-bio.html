Career Arc

A 27-Year Run in Manitoba Roofing

PRE-1995
Winnipeg roots
Educated at River East Collegiate in Winnipeg. Entered the roofing industry as a sub-contractor, learning the trade hands-on.
1995
Started sub-contracting with Don Fata
Ash Boyd & Don Fata began sub-contracting in the roofing industry — the partnership that would define both their careers.
JAN 1998
Co-founded Pristine Roofing & Siding
Incorporated Pristine Home Renovations Ltd. (later Pristine Roofing & Siding) to "ensure and raise the level of quality, service & workmanship available in the roofing & siding industry in Winnipeg." Opened at 524 Fleet Avenue.
2000s–2010s
Built the employee-based model
Pioneered a full-time, employee-based crew approach in an industry dominated by sub-contracting — giving Pristine direct quality control on every job site. Grew to 15,000+ homes serviced.
2015
Launched Pristine Custom Homes
Expanded vertically with a sister company, Pristine Custom Homes, taking the team's exterior expertise into full custom home construction. Ran it for nearly 9 years (Jan 2015 – Nov 2023).
2020+
Industry certifications & recognition
Pristine achieved BBB A+ Accreditation, COR safety certification, HAAG inspector status, and active membership in the Manitoba Home Builders' Association, Canadian Home Builders' Association, and Manitoba Shingling Contractors Association. Featured on 680 CJOB radio and the Winnipeg Business Podcast.
TODAY
$3.8M/year, 25+ years strong
Pristine is now a $3.8M annual revenue, 6-to-20-person operation (LinkedIn lists 6, RocketReach lists 13) employing full-time trades, multiple foremen, project managers, and admin staff. Serves Winnipeg + cottage country (Kenora, Lake of the Woods, Falcon Lake, Whiteshell, West Hawk Lake).
What He Built

Two Companies, One Philosophy

🏠

Pristine Roofing & Siding Ltd.

Founded 1998 · President since day one · 524 Fleet Ave, Winnipeg

Premier roofing & siding contractor — residential & commercial. Specialties: roofing, siding, eavestroughs, soffit, fascia, cladding, spray foam insulation, attic restoration, and the company's signature specialty: resolving ice damming, condensation & heat loss issues.

15,000+ homes serviced across Manitoba & Ontario cottage country. Employee-based model with full-time skilled trades — no sub-contracting on customer jobs.

🏗️

Pristine Custom Homes

Founded Jan 2015 · President · Winnipeg, MB (Jan 2015 – Nov 2023 per LinkedIn)

Sister-company vertical expansion — bringing Pristine's exterior expertise into full custom home construction. Marketed through the Pristine brand, leveraging existing reputation for quality.

The PCH brand still appears on the WinnipegRoofers.ca listing: "Custom home construction" is listed as a Pristine service line.
