A 9-9 Crossover Team Is the Most Expensive Roster in the League to Inherit

November 22, 2026 · 10:44 AM · AI-synthesized analysis

Nine wins, nine losses, fourth in the West, and a road playoff run that came, in the club's own words, "a handful of plays short" of the Grey Cup. That profile is the worst-case cap inheritance in the CFL: good enough that the front office already paid veteran money to hold it together, bad enough that a new coach will be tempted to blow it up. Five straight championship appearances don't come free, they come from retention, and retention means roster and signing bonuses already sunk into a core that just finished.500.

The damage lands on the lines, where veteran Canadian depth is the scarcest and most expensive commodity on a CFL roster. Wade Miller's stated mandate, "a change gives us the best chance to win a championship", rules out a rebuild. The new coach has to retool with contracts he didn't write, which means restructures and extensions, not cuts, before free agency opens.
