Sheahan’s 39.1 Net Is Winnipeg’s Hidden Defensive Scheme

November 23, 2026 · 06:00 AM · AI-synthesized analysis

48.5 gross minus 39.1 net equals 9.4 yards per punt given back after the catch. That gap is the punt coverage unit’s true grade, and the Bombers still finished with the CFL’s top net average among punters with more than five games. In X-and-O terms, that is not a special-teams stat; it is defensive play-calling. A 39.1 net repeatedly pushes opposing offenses into longer fields, letting Winnipeg’s defense dictate down-and-distance and keep safeties out of bad spots.

The risk is hidden: if the 9.4-yard leak widens by even a yard, the defense absorbs an extra first-down of field per exchange. Re-signing Sheahan for 2027 protects the field-position floor, but it does not fix coverage spacing. The people hurt by this number are opposing return coordinators, and Bomber fans who assume gross average is the story.
