Winnipeg beat the UFL’s Orlando rights to Roberts

November 24, 2026 · 06:00 AM · AI-synthesized analysis

Three rounds and roughly one week are the hidden numbers in the Jacob Roberts signing: Orlando Storm selected him in the third round of the UFL Draft last week, and Winnipeg announced his one-year CFL contract on Nov. 23. That timing matters more than the usual “NFL cut finds CFL home” framing. Roberts had a live alternative pro pathway, even if only draft rights, and the Bombers moved before that pathway could harden into a bidding problem. The 25-year-old linebacker is not a camp body: he posted 93 defensive tackles in 18 games for Calgary in 2025, plus nine special-teams tackles. A one-year CFL deal secures his rights now while limiting Winnipeg’s commitment if the UFL or NFL comes back. Media will file this under linebacker depth; the transaction data says Winnipeg is managing a second-league option clock.
