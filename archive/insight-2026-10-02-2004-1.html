Week 18 is the first time Winnipeg's four-receiver set has actually existed on the field

October 02, 2026 · 08:00 PM · AI-synthesized analysis

Ontaria Wilson returns from a two-game hand injury carrying 56 catches for 739 yards and five touchdowns in 12 games — 61.6 yards per game at 13.2 yards per catch — and for the first time all season he, Tim White, Nic Demski and Dorian Singer are all listed as starters together. Wilson ranks third on his own team in receiving, which tells you the depth of this room. Rookie Kenneth Womack, who played the last two games and registered zero statistics, is the corresponding demotion to the practice roster.
