Winnipeg Blue Bombers: Offensive Stalls Masking a 4-2 Record

Standings and the West Division Battle Royale

The Winnipeg Blue Bombers sit at 4-2, good for second place in the CFL's West Division as of July 23, 2025. They trail the 5-1 BC Lions by one game but hold a two-game lead over the 2-4 Saskatchewan Roughriders and a three-game cushion over the 1-5 Edmonton Elks. The Calgary Stampeders, who visit IG Field on Friday night, are 3-3 and lurking in the middle of the pack. Winnipeg's next three games are at home—against Calgary, the Ottawa RedBlacks, and the Hamilton Tiger-Cats—offering a critical window to build separation before a brutal road stretch in August. The Bombers' +14 point differential is solid but unspectacular, and their 24.3 offensive points per game ranks eighth in a nine-team league, ahead of only the winless Ottawa RedBlacks. That number is the clearest indicator of a team that wins on grit and defence, not offensive firepower.

Stats Leaders: Castillo's Boot vs. Oliveira's Grind

Kicker Sergio Castillo is the team's statistical MVP through six games, leading the CFL with 18 field goals on 20 attempts—a 90 percent conversion rate. He is on pace for 54 field goals this season, which would shatter the single-season record of 59 held by Montreal's David Cote (1997) if he maintains this volume. But that volume is the problem: the Bombers are settling for three points instead of seven. Running back Brady Oliveira has 456 rushing yards on 92 carries (4.96 yards per carry) with two touchdowns, ranking third in the league in rushing. Quarterback Zach Collaros has thrown for 1,542 yards with nine touchdowns and five interceptions, good for a 96.3 passer rating—solid but not elite. The receiving corps, led by Kenny Lawler (32 catches, 432 yards, three touchdowns) and Dalton Schoen (28 catches, 389 yards, four touchdowns), is producing yards but not finishing drives. The defence has been stingy, allowing just 22.5 points per game, third-best in the CFL, with defensive back Deatrick Nichols leading the team with three interceptions.

Matchup Preview: Calgary Stampeders at Winnipeg Blue Bombers

Friday's matchup at IG Field pits a Bombers team that is 3-0 at home this season against a Stampeders squad that is 1-2 on the road. The key battle will be in the trenches: Winnipeg's offensive line, which just released guard Micah Mazzccua after a single start, must protect Collaros against a Calgary pass rush that has 14 sacks, tied for third in the league. On the other side, the Bombers' defence, led by linebacker Adam Bighill (38 tackles), must contain Calgary running back Dedrick Mills, who has 412 rushing yards and three touchdowns. The Bombers are favoured by 4.5 points, per CFL betting lines, but the over/under of 49.5 suggests oddsmakers expect a moderate-scoring affair. If Winnipeg's offence stalls again, Castillo will be busy—and that is not a recipe for long-term success.
