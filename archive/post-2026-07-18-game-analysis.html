What emerged against Toronto on July 12 was not a scaled-down version of the same system, but a fundamentally different attack. Brown attempted 31 passes, and 22 of them were thrown within 2.5 seconds of the snap, per CFL statisticians. That’s a 71 percent quick-game rate, compared to Collaros’s season average of 48 percent. This was not dink-and-dunk; it was a surgical dissection of Toronto’s Cover-2 shell. Pierce dialed up mesh concepts and shallow crossers that put Brown’s best trait—anticipation and accuracy—on full display.
