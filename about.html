About NLK Automation

We build websites that run themselves — from Winnipeg, Manitoba.

🎯 The Mission

NLK Automation is a Winnipeg, Manitoba-based AI agency building autonomous infrastructure for Canadian local businesses. We're your hometown team for AI — founded and run out of Winnipeg, working with businesses across the Prairies and beyond. We engineer web assets that independently research industry developments, draft in-depth content, publish structured pages, and capture high-intent customer leads 24 hours a day without requiring human intervention.

"Give every local business the AI infrastructure a CFL front office would envy."
The Core Operating Standard

Websites are no longer static digital brochures that sit forgotten for months at a time. In modern commercial operations, a web presence should function as a dedicated employee that never sleeps—continuously pulling in fresh market data, optimizing search rankings, answering prospect inquiries, and expanding market reach. Being Winnipeg-based means we understand the local market — from the Exchange District to Steinbach — and build for businesses that serve these communities.

⚡ The Proof: Autonomous Operations in Production

Wade Miller Bombers is not a static concept site; it is a live, fully autonomous demonstration platform. Running four times daily, the site automatically ingests real-time Canadian Football League news, raw box scores, and roster moves, processes the dataset through DeepSeek analytical engines, and publishes formatted articles complete with schema markup, social distributions, and email digests. No human author touches the workflow.
