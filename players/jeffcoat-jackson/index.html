Bio

Jackson Jeffcoat is the other half of the CFL's most decorated pass-rush pair, bookending Willie Jefferson on a Winnipeg defensive line that has spent years making life miserable for opposing quarterbacks. At 35, he's no longer the every-down terror he was in his prime, but he remains the guy offensive coordinators have to account for on second-and-long. He's a veteran presence in a room that is about to get a new voice in it, with Mike O'Shea out and the head coaching job sitting vacant.

2026 Season Analysis

The 2026 tape is a split screen. Jeffcoat is still a pressure machine when the offence is forced to throw, bending the edge and collapsing the pocket in obvious passing downs, and yet he's part of a front that got gutted for 377 yards on the ground in the game that put the entire unit on blast. He's not the problem in run defence so much as a symptom of a group that got pushed around at the point of attack all year, and it showed in a 9-9 finish and an Eastern Final loss in Montreal. What to watch: whether the next head coach, whether that's Khari Jones, Buck Pierce, or someone else entirely, keeps Jeffcoat in a pass-rush specialist role or asks him to carry a heavier early-down load he may no longer be built for.
